About Me

A Cruise Storyteller,
Born for the Camera

Born and raised in Mexico City, Paola found her voice on cruise ships — first as a live entertainer and host, and now as a bilingual content creator building a real audience around the cruise lifestyle. As @paodecrucero, she has grown to 60K+ followers on TikTok with 400+ original videos that turn everyday onboard moments into shareable stories.

Today, as an Experience Coordinator on the Carnival Mardi Gras, she works at the intersection of hospitality, entertainment, and storytelling — spotting the moments that matter, capturing them on camera, and shaping them into platform-ready content for both her own brand and the cruise lines she loves.
